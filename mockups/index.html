How the pages connect
  • Sidebar & topbar “←” back-buttons navigate between pages.
  • Each customer ticket links up to its parent incident (and vice-versa).
  • The parent incident posts updates to the public status page automatically.
  • One bulk reply on the incident sends a message to every linked child ticket's reporter.

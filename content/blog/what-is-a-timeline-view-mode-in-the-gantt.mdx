**A timeline view mode is the zoom level of the Gantt chart, setting how wide a span of time is shown at once: a week, a month, or a quarter.** It decides the horizon you read, from a detailed day-by-day week to a rolling 13-week overview, without touching the schedule itself. Different readers need different reach, and the mode lets each one see the same plan at the altitude that suits their job. EDGEBIC by User Solutions offers week, month, and quarter timeline modes so the floor, the department, and the front office read one schedule at three scales.

## How it works

The Gantt shows scheduled operations as bars along a time axis. The timeline view mode sets how much of that axis fits on screen. Week zooms in to a single week, spreading the days out so each operation's start and finish are easy to read. Month pulls back to show a full month, compressing the days but revealing a planning span. Quarter pulls back further to a rolling 13 weeks, trading day-level detail for the shape of the workload across three months.

Changing the mode is purely a display choice. It does not reschedule anything, move a bar, or alter a single date. The plan underneath is identical whether you look at it a week or a quarter at a time; only the zoom changes.

The reason there are three modes is that different people plan at different distances. A supervisor dispatching today's work needs the week's detail. A department manager balancing load across the coming weeks needs the month. A plant manager judging where the busy periods fall needs the quarter. One schedule, three natural reading distances.

## A concrete example

Think of a mapping app. When you are walking the last block to a destination, you zoom in to street level and see individual turns. When you are planning a cross-town trip, you zoom out to see the neighborhoods. When you are deciding whether to drive to another city, you zoom out again to see the whole region. The map is the same; the zoom matches the decision.

The Gantt works the same way. Zoomed to week, a supervisor sees that Job 42 mills Monday morning and drills Monday afternoon, precise enough to run the shift. Zoomed to month, a manager sees the general flow of jobs across four weeks. Zoomed to quarter, the plant manager sees which of the next 13 weeks are heavy and which are light. Each reader picks the mode that answers their question.

**To count only primary hours in job totals in EDGEBIC, open Settings and then the Schedule tab, tick Primary hours only (exclude parallel) in the Job-Level Hours group, and click Save.** In EDGEBIC by User Solutions this stops synchronized parallel machines from double counting: with the setting on, job and routing totals count the primary steps only, so three mirrored drill heads that log 24 machine-hours between them report as the 8 hours of work the job actually represents.

This post covers one roll-up setting. For the whole Settings and Schedule policy screen, see [how to configure scheduling policy in EDGEBIC](/blog/how-to-configure-scheduling-policy-in-edgebic); the full task library is at the [EDGEBIC how-to hub](/blog/edgebic-how-to-hub).

## Why the totals double count

A dependent-parallel routing runs several machines in lockstep on one operation. One work center is the primary, and the others mirror it exactly: same start, same end, each carrying its share of the work. That is the correct physical model, and each mirrored machine genuinely occupies its own capacity for the duration. The catch is at the job level. By default the job total adds up every step, primary and mirrored alike, so an operation run across three synchronized drill heads reports the sum of all three, 24 machine-hours, when the job it belongs to represents only 8 hours of work. Primary hours only tells the roll-up to count the primary path and leave the mirrors out of the total, so the number a planner or finance reads matches the work the job actually stands for.

## Before You Start

- You use dependent-parallel routings, where several machines mirror one operation and run in lockstep. If you do not, this setting has nothing to change.
- You can reach the **Settings** tab; it is permission-controlled.
- You understand this is a roll-up display choice, not a scheduling change.

## The Steps

1. **Open the Schedule policy tab.** From the main navigation open **Settings**, then the **Schedule** sub-tab. This is the site-wide scheduling policy screen.
2. **Find the Job-Level Hours group.** Scroll to the group labeled **Job-Level Hours**.
3. **Tick the option.** Turn on **Primary hours only (exclude parallel)**. Its default is off.
4. **Save.** Click the **Save** button at the bottom. The status line confirms the save.
5. **Check the affected screens.** The change applies where hours roll up: BOR header totals, Job View, and reports.

## What Changes When You Save

| Surface | Effect |
|---|---|
| BOR header totals | Count the primary path, not every mirrored parallel machine. |
| Job View totals | Read the work the job represents rather than the sum across parallel machines. |
| Reports | Roll up primary hours where job and routing totals appear. |
| Work-center load and capacity | Unchanged. Each parallel machine still shows its own hours. |
| The schedule | Unchanged. No operation moves; only the roll-up changes. |

## How to Check It Worked

Open a job that uses a dependent-parallel routing in **Job View** and read its total hours. With Primary hours only on, the total should equal the primary path, for example 8 hours for an operation mirrored across three drill heads, rather than the summed 24. Turn the setting off and Save to see the total return to the full machine-hour tally, then turn it back on. If the total flips between the two values, the setting is working.

## Common Mistakes

**Expecting capacity to change too.** This setting is about job totals, not work-center load. Each mirrored machine still consumes and shows its own capacity, because it genuinely runs. Only the job-level roll-up changes.

**Turning it on when you have no parallel routings.** With no dependent-parallel steps there is nothing to exclude, so the totals look identical. The setting matters only when several machines mirror one operation.

**Reading a changed total as a changed plan.** No operation moves when you flip this. If a job total drops after you tick the box, that is the roll-up correcting, not the schedule shifting.

**Forgetting to Save.** Policy settings are read at the next roll-up. Tick the box and click Save, or the totals will not update.

**To replace a work center in a scenario in EDGEBIC, open the quote's Scenarios view, add a step override, set the Override Type to Replace, choose the Alternative Work Center, and click Run Simulation.** The step then runs on the new machine inside that scenario only. The real routing and the live schedule never change.

A scenario in EDGEBIC by User Solutions is a named what-if variant of a quote's simulation: same product and quantity, but with deliberately altered conditions. A Replace step override is the lever for testing "what if this operation ran somewhere else," whether that is a second in-house machine or an outside vendor, without disturbing anything real.

## Before You Start

- **A quote exists and has been simulated once.** Scenarios are children of a quote, and the base simulation is your comparison baseline. See [how to create a what-if scenario](/blog/how-to-create-a-what-if-scenario-in-edgebic).
- **You know which step and which machine.** Replace needs the routing step and the alternative work center it should run on.
- **You understand the honest limit.** Replace changes the machine, not the transit or vendor lead time, unless the alternative work center carries different times of its own.
- **You keep a baseline scenario.** A no-override Standard scenario is what you compare the reroute against.

## Step 1: Open the Scenario View

On the **Quote** tab, select the quote you want to explore and click **Scenarios**. The What-If Scenario Analysis view opens. Select an existing scenario or click **Create New Scenario** and name it after the lever, for example `Mill step to CNC-Mill-2`.

## Step 2: Add a Step Override

In the **Production Step Overrides** group, click **Add Step Override**. A new override row appears.

## Step 3: Pick the Step and Set Replace

Choose the **Step** (the routing step you want to reroute), then set **Override Type** to **Replace**. The **Alternative Work Center** field becomes relevant.

| Override Type | Effect in this scenario | You also fill in |
|---|---|---|
| **Replace** | The step runs on a different machine | **Alternative Work Center** |

## Step 4: Choose the Alternative Work Center

Select the machine the step should run on for this scenario. This is the whole point of Replace: the operation still happens, and downstream steps still wait for it, but on the machine you named.

## Step 5: Run the Simulation

Click **Run Simulation**. Read the **Simulation Results** group: **Estimated Start Date**, **Estimated End Date**, **Lead Time**, **Total Hours**, and **Estimated Cost**. Re-run as often as you like; each run overwrites this scenario's stored results and nothing touches the shop plan.

## What Changes When You Save

| Action | What changes | What does NOT change |
|---|---|---|
| **Run Simulation** | The scenario's stored dates, hours, and cost | The production schedule, the quote, the real routing |
| **Apply to Quote** | The parent quote's estimated numbers | The product's real BOR and work centers |

## How to Check It Worked

1. Confirm the override row shows **Replace** and the alternative work center you chose.
2. Click **Run Simulation** and note the new **Estimated End Date**.
3. Open the **Comparison Analysis** tab, tick this scenario and a no-override Standard scenario, and read the Lead Time chart. The reroute should show a different bar.
4. Confirm the real routing is unchanged: open the product's routing in the BOR editor and verify the step still points at its original work center.

## Common Mistakes

**Using Replace when the step disappears.** If the operation is genuinely eliminated, use Skip instead. Replace keeps the step and its dependencies.

**Expecting vendor transit for free.** Replace swaps the machine only. If an outside vendor adds days of transit, model that in the real routing or lengthen the step with a ModifyTime override, or the scenario will look too fast.

**Forgetting to re-simulate.** The override does nothing until you click Run Simulation. A scenario that was never simulated cannot be applied to the quote.

**Treating the applied scenario as a real change.** Applying a winning scenario copies its numbers onto the quote. It does not edit the routing. Make the real change in the BOR editor before converting the quote.

**To build a weekly operator roster in EDGEBIC by User Solutions, select the operator, open the Roster sub-tab, add a global shift row, and tick the weekday columns the operator works that shift.** Each tick saves immediately. The roster is a weekly pattern, the same shape as a shift's own weekly hours: you set the normal week once and record absences separately as time off. It is what turns a certified operator into available capacity the scheduler can staff.

This post is the rostering task. It sits between certifying people and applying the constraint to a routing step, all of which are walked through in [how to set up operators, skills, and rosters](/blog/how-to-set-up-operators-skills-and-rosters-in-edgebic).

## Before You Start

| Prerequisite | Why |
|---|---|
| Global shifts exist, for example Day Shift and Night Shift | The roster is a shift-by-weekday grid, and it keys on the global shifts |
| The operator exists and is certified in at least one skill | An unrostered or uncertified operator cannot be staffed |
| Work centers using custom calendars derive them from a global shift | A person can only cover a work center shift that traces back to a global shift they are rostered on |

## Step 1: Open the Operator's Roster Sub-Tab

Open the **Daily Hours** tab, select the **Operators** inner tab, select the operator in the list, and open the **Roster** sub-tab. The roster grid holds this one person's weekly pattern.

## Step 2: Add a Shift Row

At the bottom of the grid, pick a shift in the **Add shift** selector, for example Day Shift, and click **Add Row**. A new row appears for that shift with unticked weekday columns. The shifts in the selector are the global shifts, which is why one roster row can cover every machine that mirrors that shift.

## Step 3: Tick the Weekdays Worked

On the new row, tick the weekday columns the operator works that shift: **Mo Tu We Th Fr Sa Su**. Each tick saves immediately, so there is no separate save step. A standard weekday worker on days is Day Shift with Mo through Fr ticked and Sa, Su clear.

## Step 4: Add Rows for a Rotation

If the person rotates between shifts, add another row for the second shift and tick its weekdays. Someone on days early in the week and nights later is two rows: Day Shift with Mo, Tu, We and Night Shift with Th, Fr. Use **Remove** to drop a shift row entirely. This captures the normal pattern once; a specific week off the pattern is time off, not a roster edit.

## What Changes When You Roster

| Surface | Effect |
|---|---|
| The qualified pool | On each rostered shift and weekday, the person's hours join the pool for the skills they hold |
| Skill-gated steps | From the next scheduling run, they can be placed into windows this person now staffs |
| The operator calendar | The person's availability shows on the rostered shifts and days |
| Scheduling | Nothing moves until the next run: rostering is master data |

People are budgeted in hours per shift per day. Joe holds one budget of hours on Monday's Day Shift regardless of how many skills he carries, so booking him through one skill leaves fewer hours for another. That hour accounting is described in [how EDGEBIC books operator hours](/blog/how-edgebic-books-operator-hours).

## How to Check It Worked

The roster grid shows the ticked weekdays for each shift row, and after a scheduling run the person appears in the qualified pool for their skills on those days, staffing steps that need them. If a machine still refuses to staff the person, check that the machine's shift derives from a global shift the person is rostered on.

## Common Mistakes

- **Rostering onto a work center's custom shift with no global parent.** No roster row can match it, so skill-gated steps routed there never staff. Derive the shift from a global parent.
- **Editing the roster for a one-week absence.** The roster is the normal pattern. Record a specific absence as time off instead.
- **Expecting the roster to set clock times.** It sets presence by weekday. The hours come from the shift, and overlap within a shift is normal.
- **Rostering someone who holds no certification.** Presence without a skill staffs nothing. Certify first.

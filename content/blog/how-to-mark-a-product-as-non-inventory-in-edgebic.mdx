**To mark a product as non-inventory in EDGEBIC by User Solutions, open the product, uncheck Is Stocked, and save.** Clearing that flag tells the planner to skip ledger posting, netting, and replenishment for the product, which is what a service item or overhead SKU needs. The product drops out of the inventory matrix, and the scheduler stops posting receipts and issues for its manufacturing orders. This is a permanent configuration choice, not a temporary hold.

This is one product setting. To change how a stocked product is built instead, see [how to switch a product between make to stock and make to order](/blog/how-to-switch-a-product-between-make-to-stock-and-make-to-order-in-edgebic). The full task set is on the [EDGEBIC how-to hub](/blog/edgebic-how-to-hub).

## Before You Start

- The product exists. The flag is a field on the product record.
- Confidence that the item genuinely never holds stock, since this is a permanent setting.
- If the product is make-to-stock, a plan to change its build method too, because the two settings cannot both be true.

## Step by Step

1. Open the **Products** tab and edit the product.
2. Find the **Is Stocked** checkbox in the inventory planning area.
3. Uncheck **Is Stocked**.
4. Click **Save**.

## What Changes When You Save

With Is Stocked off, the projection and netting engine skip the product entirely. It no longer appears in the inventory matrix, and the scheduler no longer posts ledger receipts or issues for its manufacturing orders. Replenishment suggestions stop for it as well, because there is no balance to replenish. The product remains active and can still appear on routings and jobs, so this affects inventory handling only, not whether the item can be used.

If the product is also set to make-to-stock, EDGEBIC flags the combination with an anomaly check, because a make-to-stock item is supposed to post finished-goods receipts to a balance it no longer keeps. Resolve the contradiction before relying on the setting.

## How to Check It Worked

Open the inventory calendar or matrix and confirm the product no longer appears as a stocked row. Schedule and complete one of its jobs, then check that no receipt or issue was posted to a ledger for the item. If the item still shows up in the matrix, confirm the flag saved on the correct product. If an anomaly is flagged, check whether the product is still set to make-to-stock and adjust the build method.

## Common Mistakes

- **Using it as a temporary hold.** This is a permanent flag. To pause a product's jobs, deactivate it or hold its orders instead.
- **Leaving make-to-stock on.** A make-to-stock, non-inventory product is contradictory and gets flagged. Change the build method too.
- **Expecting history to vanish.** Turning the flag off later restores historical entries to the running balance, so records are preserved.
- **Confusing it with deactivation.** Non-inventory keeps the item usable but unstocked. Deactivation retires the item from new use.

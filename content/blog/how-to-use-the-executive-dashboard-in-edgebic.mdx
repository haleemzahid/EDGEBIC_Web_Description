**The exec dashboard tab in EDGEBIC by User Solutions gives a 90-day operational view: hero tiles for throughput, on-time-in-full, margin, and backlog, a multi-plant rollup by department, and top and bottom product lists, each figure carrying a chip that states how far to trust it.** It is the tab you put in front of a general manager or director when the question is direction, not detail.

It sits alongside the Standup and Planner tabs inside one cockpit, and the tour of all four is in [the EDGEBIC dashboard tabs](/blog/how-to-use-the-edgebic-dashboard-tabs). The idea that every figure declares its own provenance runs through [EDGEBIC dashboards explained](/blog/edgebic-dashboards-explained). Every task in this library is mapped on the [EDGEBIC how-to hub](/blog/edgebic-how-to-hub).

## Before You Start

- Orders exist and are scheduled. Throughput, on-time-in-full, and backlog all read from your orders and their schedules.
- Work centers have their department assigned if you want the multi-plant rollup to attribute jobs correctly.
- You accept the dashboard does not auto-refresh. Click Refresh now before a review to pull current numbers.

## Step 1: Open the Exec Tab

Open the Dashboard section from the main navigation and click the **Exec** tab. It loads in the light office theme. Because there is no auto-refresh timer, click **Refresh now** before you present, and watch the last-updated time stamp move.

## Step 2: Read the Four Hero Tiles

The hero row is the headline:

- **Throughput**, the quantity completed over the last seven days.
- **On-time-in-full**, the share of completed orders that landed by their due date over the window.
- **Margin**, which currently shows a dash because revenue is not captured.
- **Backlog**, the count and quantity of orders not yet complete.

Read each tile's chip alongside its value. Backlog carries a Live chip because it is a persisted fact. On-time-in-full carries a Plan chip because it uses projected end dates until actuals fully replace them. Margin carries an Offline chip and a dash rather than a fabricated figure.

## Step 3: Read the Multi-Plant Rollup

The rollup shows one row per department, attributing each job to the single department whose stations consumed the most of its scheduled hours, then aggregating quantity and on-time counts into a throughput-per-week figure. It is the fastest read on which plant or area is carrying the load and which is falling behind.

## Step 4: Read Top and Bottom Products

Two product lists close the tab. Top products rank by volume over the window. Bottom products rank by on-time performance, worst first, with the highest-volume laggards floating to the top. The bottom list is your shortlist for a product-level investigation.

## What the Chips Are Telling You

The data-quality chip is the honesty layer. Live is a persisted fact. Plan is a scheduler projection that will firm up as actuals arrive. Estimate is a heuristic. Offline means the figure cannot be computed yet, which is why margin shows a dash. Read the chip before you quote a number in a board pack: a Plan figure is directionally sound but not yet a hard fact, and the info icon on each tile explains exactly how it is calculated, which is the routine in [how to check where a dashboard number comes from](/blog/how-to-check-where-a-dashboard-number-comes-from-in-edgebic).

## How to Check It Worked

The tab is read-only, so nothing is saved. Confirm it worked by checking that backlog matches the count of open orders you expect and that the last-updated stamp reflects your refresh. If a department's throughput looks too low, confirm all its work centers have a department assigned before concluding the output is genuinely down.

## Common Mistakes

- **Reading a Plan figure as a hard fact.** On-time-in-full is projection-based until actuals are fully logged. Note the chip when you quote it.
- **Treating the margin dash as a bug.** It is an honest Offline state because revenue is not captured. The tile refuses to invent a number.
- **Forgetting to refresh.** There is no auto-refresh. A stale tab shows the last time it was activated, not now.
- **Trusting the rollup with unmapped stations.** A department missing on some work centers drops those jobs from its total. Confirm the assignments before reading a low number as real.

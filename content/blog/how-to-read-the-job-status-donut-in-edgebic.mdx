**The job status donut in EDGEBIC by User Solutions lives on the Capacity dashboard tab, in the Overview area's Active Alerts panel: it sorts every active job into one of four colored slices, Late, Overdue, On time, or Early, so you can see the health of the whole order book at a glance.** Each job lands in exactly one slice, and the list beside the donut ranks them worst first.

The donut is one signal on the Capacity tab, which also carries the capacity load view covered in [reading a red day on the capacity view](/blog/reading-a-red-day-on-the-edgebic-capacity-view). Misreading dashboard signals is common enough to warrant its own guide in [dashboard misreading mistakes](/blog/dashboard-misreading-mistakes-edgebic). Every task in this library is mapped on the [EDGEBIC how-to hub](/blog/edgebic-how-to-hub).

## Before You Start

- Jobs are scheduled. The donut classifies against the schedule, so an unscheduled job has nothing to place.
- Due dates are set. The four buckets are all defined relative to the due date.
- You have refreshed the tab. The dashboard does not auto-update, so a stale donut reflects the last time the tab was activated.

## Step 1: Open the Capacity Tab

Open the Dashboard section from the main navigation. The Capacity tab is the default landing tab. In the Overview area, find the Active Alerts panel; the job status donut sits there with the Active Jobs list beside it.

## Step 2: Read the Four Slices

Each slice is a schedule-margin bucket, and only active jobs are counted:

| Slice | Color | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Late | Red | The job finished after its due date, or its current plan overruns the due date, flagged even before the due date arrives |
| Overdue | Amber | The plan meets the due date, but the due date has already passed with the job unfinished |
| On time | Green | The due date is still open and the plan lands exactly on it, or the job finished on or before it |
| Early | Teal | The due date is still open and the plan finishes before it, with slack |

## Step 3: Work the Active Jobs List

The list beside the donut sorts worst first, so red Late jobs sit at the top. Each row carries an In progress badge when the job has physically started, which is shown separately from the slice because starting a job does not change whether it is on time. Read the badge and the slice together: a started job in the Late slice is running and behind, a Late job with no badge has not begun and is already structurally late.

## Step 4: Read the Ready Date

The donut and the list classify against the delivery-ready end, which is the scheduled end plus the product's end-item lead time, and show a Ready date. This is why the buckets agree with the days-late column on the Drive Schedule grid: both account for the lead time a product needs after its last operation, not just the raw scheduled end.

## How to Check It Worked

The donut is a read-only view, so nothing is saved. Confirm it worked by checking that a job you know is comfortably ahead reads Early or On time, and that a job whose plan overruns its due date reads Late even though its due date is still in the future. If the donut disagrees with the Drive Schedule grid, refresh the tab; the two are computed on the same delivery-ready basis and should line up.

## Common Mistakes

- **Reading On time as having a buffer.** On time means the plan lands exactly on the due date with no slack. Early is the only bucket with real margin.
- **Expecting a started job to change slice.** Starting a job shows the In progress badge but does not move it between buckets. The slice is about dates, not activity.
- **Confusing Late with Overdue.** Late is a plan overrunning the due date, which can be true today. Overdue is a due date already past. Late is the earlier warning.
- **Trusting a stale donut.** The tab does not auto-refresh. Refresh before you read it against a fresh schedule run.

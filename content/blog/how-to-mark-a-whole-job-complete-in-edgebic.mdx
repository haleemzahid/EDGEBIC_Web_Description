**To mark a whole job complete in EDGEBIC by User Solutions, click Complete this Job on the Job View tab, enter the completion date and an optional reason, and click OK.** This is an order-level action, separate from closing individual operations. It locks actuals entry, banners the job as completed, and shades its bars. The roll-up tells you when a job is ready to close, but closing it is still a deliberate click.

Completing a single operation is a different task, covered in [how to complete an operation](/blog/how-to-complete-an-operation-in-edgebic). This is the whole order.

## Before You Start

| Prerequisite | Why |
|---|---|
| Every operation is done | The roll-up reports 100 percent only when all steps have finished |
| The completion date is known | The Mark Complete dialog asks for it |
| You are ready to reschedule the rest | Closing a job frees the capacity it was holding, which the remaining plan should pick up |

## Step 1: Confirm the Roll-Up Is Ready

Open the **Job View** tab and select the job. The header carries the job progress figure. It reaches 100 percent only when every operation is done, because a job reports an actual end for the whole order only after its last operation closes. Finishing one step early never flags the whole job, which is exactly what keeps the Job Progress report and the dashboards honest.

If the roll-up is short of 100 percent, an operation is still open. Close it first: [how to complete an operation](/blog/how-to-complete-an-operation-in-edgebic) covers the per-step stamp, and [how to record a partially finished operation](/blog/how-to-record-a-partially-finished-operation-in-edgebic) covers a step that finished short.

## Step 2: Click Complete this Job

1. Click **Complete this Job** on the Job View tab.
2. The **Mark Complete** dialog asks for a completion date and an optional reason.
3. Click **OK**.

The header now shows the completion banner reading that the job is completed on that date, the **Log Actual** and **Complete this Job** buttons lock, and the job's bars take the completed shade on the Gantt.

## Step 3: Reschedule the Rest of the Plant

Closing the order releases the capacity it was reserving. Run a reschedule for the remaining open jobs so that freed capacity flows into the plan, rather than leaving other work queued behind a job that is already off the floor. The safe procedure is in [how to reschedule safely](/blog/how-to-reschedule-safely-in-edgebic).

## What Changes When You Complete the Job

| Surface | Effect |
|---|---|
| The job header | Shows the completed-on banner |
| The logging buttons | Lock: no further actuals entry until the job is reopened |
| The Gantt bars | Take the completed shade |
| The audit trail | An order-level completion event is appended |
| Build-to-stock inventory | On-hand increases by the build quantity as the finished goods are recognized |
| The next reschedule | The engine recognizes the order as done and writes it back unchanged |

## How to Reopen a Job

If the completion was premature, click **Reopen Job** on the Job View tab. The banner clears, the logging buttons unlock, and actuals entry is available again. For a build-to-stock order, reopening reverses the finished-goods receipt so on-hand returns to where it was.

One thing reopening does not do: it does not clear the actual dates on the individual steps. The order returns to a schedulable state, but each operation keeps whatever actual start and end it had. If you need to fully reset the job to not-started, clear the per-step actuals after reopening, which is [how to reopen a completed operation](/blog/how-to-reopen-a-completed-operation-in-edgebic) applied step by step.

## How to Check It Worked

The header banner reads that the job is completed on the date you entered, and the **Complete this Job** and **Log Actual** buttons are disabled. On the Gantt, every bar carries the completed shade. Open **Job Audit** and confirm the newest event is the order-level completion with your completion date. For a build-to-stock order, check that on-hand for the finished item rose by the build quantity. Reading the trail is covered in [how to see who changed a schedule](/blog/how-to-see-who-changed-a-schedule-in-edgebic).

## Common Mistakes

- **Trying to close the order with an operation still open.** The roll-up stays short of 100 percent and the completion cannot certify a job that is not finished. Close the operations first.
- **Treating the roll-up figure as the completion.** Reporting 100 percent means ready to close, not closed. The order-level stamp is still a click you make.
- **Completing a build-to-stock job before the parts exist.** On-hand rises for finished goods that are not really finished. Close the order when the last operation is genuinely done.
- **Forgetting to reschedule after closing.** The rest of the plant keeps scheduling around capacity the finished job no longer needs.
- **Reopening and expecting a full reset.** Reopening unlocks the order but leaves step-level actuals in place. Clear those separately if a clean slate is the goal.

**Makespan is the total span from the moment the first operation in a set of jobs starts to the moment the last operation finishes.** It measures how long a whole batch of work takes to clear the floor. A shorter makespan means the plant finishes the same workload sooner and releases its resources earlier for the next batch.

[EDGEBIC by User Solutions](/edgebic) computes makespan as a scheduling KPI and can optimize for it directly. This article defines the term and shows how job sequence changes it.

## How It Works

Makespan is a whole-schedule measure, not a per-job one. Take every operation across every job in the run, find the earliest start of any of them, find the latest finish of any of them, and the span between those two points is the makespan. It answers a simple question: from the first tool touching metal to the last part leaving the floor, how long did all of this take?

The number is driven by how jobs share work centers. When several jobs compete for the same machines, the sequence in which they claim those machines decides how much idle time creeps into the schedule. A poor sequence leaves expensive stations waiting; a good sequence keeps them fed. Because the workload is identical either way, makespan is one of the clearest measures of pure scheduling quality.

## A Concrete Example

Take two work centers, a cutting station and a milling station, one machine each, sharing a single day shift. Three jobs are all released Monday morning. Job A cuts for six hours then mills for two. Job B cuts for two hours then mills for six. Job C cuts and mills for three hours each.

Schedule them in the order A, B, C and the mill sits idle all Monday morning while A's long cut blocks the saw. The batch does not finish until Wednesday morning, a makespan of about 17 working hours. Now sequence them B, A, C: B's short two-hour cut feeds the mill by mid-morning, the mill stays busy, and all three jobs finish Tuesday afternoon, a makespan of about 14 working hours. Same three jobs, same two machines, three working hours saved purely from the order.

**To add a new product in EDGEBIC by User Solutions, open the Product tab, click Add Product, enter a Product Id, and click Save.** That is the whole required path. The Product Configuration dialog carries a dozen more fields, but only the Id is mandatory, and the product appears in every dropdown in the application the moment you save. This post covers the single task of creating the record. For the full field-by-field tour, read [how to set up products in EDGEBIC](/blog/how-to-set-up-products-in-edgebic).

## Before you start

- You are signed in and can see the **Product** tab on the main navigation.
- You have permission to add products. The **Add Product** button is permission-gated, so ask your administrator if it is missing.
- Optional but useful: the unit of measure and the category you intend to assign already exist. Both can also be created on the fly from the product form.
- Decide the identifier before you open the dialog. Renaming later is possible but touches routings and scheduled jobs.

## Steps

1. Open the **Product** tab from the main navigation. The **Product Management** page opens with the grid grouped by category.
2. Click **Add Product** at the top right. The **Product Configuration** dialog opens with the heading **Add Product**.
3. Enter the **Product Id**. This is the required field and the name planners will read everywhere: on the routing, on the manufacturing order, on the quote, and on the Gantt. Keep it short and readable, such as `Widget-A` or `Frame-S`.
4. Leave **Product Name** to auto-generate, or overtype it with your formal part number. This secondary name must be unique across products.
5. Pick the **Unit of Measure** from the dropdown. It reads "select unit" until you choose one.
6. Enter **Sale Price** if you sell the item, and **Rollup Cost** if you quote it. These two feed quote totals and margin, so a quoted product with blank values produces a misleading margin figure.
7. Enter **Lead Time** in calendar days if the item needs a finishing window after the last operation, such as packing or paint curing. Leave it at zero when there is no post-production tail. See [how to change a product's lead time](/blog/how-to-change-a-products-lead-time-in-edgebic) for what that number actually does to a job's end date.
8. Assign a **Category**. Click **New** beside the dropdown to create one without leaving the form, or set it up in advance using [how to create a product category](/blog/how-to-create-a-product-category-in-edgebic).
9. Fill the descriptive fields you care about: **Size**, **Description**, **Weight**, **Country of Origin**, and an optional **Icon/Image** picked with **Browse**.
10. Click **Save**. The dialog closes and the row appears in the grid.

## What changes when you save

The product record exists and is immediately selectable. Product dropdowns across the application refresh without a restart or a manual reload, which includes the manufacturing order dialog, the quote dialog, and the routing module's product palette.

Nothing else moves. No capacity is booked, no job is created, and no existing schedule is touched. The new row appears in the grid with a circle marker beside its identifier and **No BOR** in the **BOR** column, which is the expected state for a product whose routing has not been built yet.

| Surface | Effect of saving a new product |
|---|---|
| Product dropdowns (orders, quotes, routing module) | Available at once, no refresh needed |
| Product grid | New row, grouped under its category, marked **No BOR** |
| Quotes | The product becomes quotable once it has a routing and costs |
| The schedule | Nothing moves. Product records never trigger a reschedule |

## How to check it worked

1. Find the new row in the **Product** grid. Type a fragment of the Id into the search panel above the columns if the catalog is long.
2. Confirm the **BOR** column reads **No BOR** and the marker beside the identifier is a circle. That is correct for a brand-new product and tells you the next task is the routing.
3. Open the manufacturing order dialog and confirm the product appears in its dropdown. If it does not, the save did not complete.
4. If you entered a **Sale Price** below the **Rollup Cost**, expect the warning "Unit price is less than unit cost. You may be selling at a loss." It does not block the save, but it is worth confirming both numbers are right.

## Common mistakes

**Leaving Product Id blank or too short.** The dialog rejects the save with "Product id is required." The Id needs at least two characters.

**Reusing a Product Name.** The secondary name is enforced unique through the form. If you see "is already in use" under **Product Name**, choose a different value or clear it and let the auto-generated one stand.

**Treating the new product as ready to order.** A product with no routing cannot be scheduled. The grid's **BOR** column is the fastest place to spot the gap, and you can group the whole catalog by routing readiness with a single click on that column. See [how to find which products have no routing](/blog/how-to-find-which-products-have-no-routing-in-edgebic).

**Padding Lead Time to buy safety.** Lead time is meant for genuine post-production time. Inflating it hides capacity problems in master data and makes every promise date later than it needs to be.

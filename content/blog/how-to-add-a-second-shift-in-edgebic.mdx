**To add a second shift in EDGEBIC, open the Daily Hours tab, select the inner Shift tab, and click Add Shift.** Name it, tick the weekdays it runs, set the start and end times, and save. Then confirm it reached the work centers you meant it to reach, because that second step is where most of the capacity gets lost.

Adding hours is the single largest lever on a promise date. A one-instance work center on one 8-hour shift offers 8 hours a day. Attach a second 8-hour shift and it offers 16. This page covers that one task from end to end. For the whole calendar model (shifts, holidays, downtime, overrides and how they stack), read [how to set up shifts and holidays in EDGEBIC by User Solutions](/blog/how-to-set-up-shifts-holidays-downtime-in-edgebic).

## Before You Start

- **You know the real hours the second crew works**, including the days it does not run.
- **You know which work centers the shift applies to.** Not every machine gets staffed on nights.
- **You know which of those work centers are on a custom calendar.** Those need the shift added individually.
- **You can rerun the schedule afterward.** The new hours do nothing until Drive Schedule runs.

## Step 1: Open the Shift Management Page

Open the **Daily Hours** tab from the main navigation, then select the inner **Shift** tab. The **Shift Management** page lists your existing shifts with columns **S.#**, **Shift Name**, **Daily Working Hours**, **Total Hours**, and **Actions**.

Read the **Total Hours** column on the shift you already have. That is the weekly number you are about to add to.

## Step 2: Click Add Shift

Click **Add Shift**. The **Shift Configuration** dialog opens with the heading **Add Shift**.

## Step 3: Name It for When It Runs

Enter the name in **Shift Name**. Name shifts by when they run, not by who works them: `Night Shift`, `Second Shift`, `Weekend Shift`. This name appears on every capacity screen and on every allocation record, so it has to be self-explanatory to someone reading a report six months from now.

## Step 4: Set the Days and the Hours

In the **Daily Working Hours** section, tick the checkbox on each day the shift runs, then set **Start:** and **End:** for that day. Each row shows the computed duration (⏱) as you type, which is your first check that the entry is right.

A standard second shift: Monday to Friday ticked, **Start:** `16:00`, **End:** `00:00`, each row showing 8h. Saturday and Sunday left unticked, contributing zero.

Days do not have to match. A shift can run 10 hours Monday to Thursday and 6 on Friday. **Reset All Days** clears every day back to 00:00 if you want to start over.

## Step 5: Save the Shift

Click **Save Shift** (💾). The dialog closes and the new shift appears in the grid with its weekly **Total Hours**.

## Step 6: Confirm It Reached the Right Work Centers

This is the step people skip. Open the **Workcenter** tab and read the **Shift Calendar** column:

| Badge | What happens to your new shift |
|---|---|
| 🌐 **Global** (green) | The work center inherits the new shift automatically. Nothing more to do |
| 📅 **Custom** (amber) | The work center follows only its own shift list. Your new shift does **not** apply until you add it there |

For a Custom work center that should work the new shift, click ✏️ **Edit**, go to the **Associated Shifts** tab, use **Add Existing Shift**, tick the new shift, click **Add Selected**, and **Save**. The mechanics are in [giving one work center its own calendar](/blog/how-to-give-one-work-center-its-own-calendar-in-edgebic).

## What Changes When You Save

| Surface | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Shift Management** grid | Updates immediately with the new **Total Hours** |
| Work centers on **Global** | Gain the hours as available capacity at the next scheduling run |
| Work centers on **Custom** | Unaffected until you add the shift to their own list |
| Resource Calendar and dashboards | Show the higher capacity when refreshed |
| Existing schedules | **Unchanged** until you run **Drive Schedule** |

## How to Check It Worked

1. Confirm the new row on the **Shift Management** grid, with the **Total Hours** you expected (40 for five 8-hour days).
2. Open the Resource Calendar and read a work center's total capacity for a normal day. A one-instance machine on two 8-hour shifts should read 16 hours where it read 8.
3. Run **Drive Schedule** and compare finish dates. A 20-hour operation that took three days on one shift should now finish inside two.
4. Open the Gantt and confirm bars appear inside the new window. If capacity rose but nothing moved, the schedule was never rerun.

## Common Mistakes

**Assuming a work center works every shift.** Capacity comes from the pairing of a work center and a shift, not from the shift alone. A machine attached to only one shift is dark during the other, no matter how many shifts the plant has defined.

**Adding the shift and never rerunning.** Saving a shift never moves a schedule. The dashboards will show more capacity while the Gantt shows the old plan, which reads like a bug and is not one.

**Editing the existing shift instead of adding a new one.** If you widen Day Shift from 08:00 to 00:00 rather than creating a second shift, you lose the ability to see which crew is loaded and you change the calendar for every work center using it. Two named shifts keep the allocations readable.

**Overlapping the two shifts by accident.** Ending the first at 16:30 and starting the second at 16:00 double-counts half an hour of clock on every working day. Read the durations on both cards before saving.

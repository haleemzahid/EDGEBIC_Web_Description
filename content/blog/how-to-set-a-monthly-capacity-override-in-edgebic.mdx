**To set a monthly capacity override in EDGEBIC by User Solutions, open the work center, go to its Monthly Overrides panel, click Add, and set a Start Date, End Date, Capacity in hours per shift, and an optional reason, then save.** The override applies a blanket capacity to every day in that range, replacing the shift formula. It is the right tool for a sustained change across many days, such as reduced summer hours, where overriding each day individually would be tedious. A daily override still wins over it for any specific date.

This is the date-range sibling of the single-day override. For one day only, see [how to override capacity for one day](/blog/how-to-override-capacity-for-one-day-in-edgebic). The full task set is on the [EDGEBIC how-to hub](/blog/edgebic-how-to-hub).

## Before You Start

- The work center exists and is saved.
- The date range you want to cover, and the capacity in hours per shift for that range.
- An understanding of precedence: a daily override wins over a monthly one, which wins over the shift formula.

## Step by Step

1. Open the **Workcenter** tab and select the work center.
2. Open its **Monthly Overrides** panel or tab.
3. Click **Add** to open the override editor.
4. Set **Start Date** and **End Date** for the range.
5. Enter **Capacity** as hours per shift for the range.
6. Enter an optional **Reason**, for example summer reduced crew.
7. Click **Save**.

## What Changes When You Save

A monthly override row now covers the date range you set. On the next scheduling run, every day in that range uses your capacity value instead of the shift formula, unless a daily override exists on a specific day, in which case the daily value wins there. The precedence is fixed: daily override first, monthly override second, shift formula last. Overlapping monthly ranges are not permitted, so saving a range that overlaps an existing one replaces the conflicting rows rather than stacking two rates on the same day.

The override affects only this work center. Other machines keep their own capacity unless you set overrides for them too.

## How to Check It Worked

Open the capacity view or a dashboard for the work center and confirm the days in your range show the override capacity rather than the formula value. Run Drive Schedule and check that jobs on this machine are planned against the reduced or increased hours for those dates. If one day in the range still shows the old number, it likely has a daily override taking precedence, which you can reset from the per-day capacity dialog.

## Common Mistakes

- **Entering per-machine hours instead of per-shift capacity.** The capacity is the value the scheduler uses for the shift. Enter what the day should have, not a per-instance figure.
- **Fighting a leftover daily override.** A daily override on a date inside the range wins. Reset it if you want the monthly rate there.
- **Trying to overlap two monthly ranges.** Overlaps are not allowed and get replaced. Split into non-overlapping ranges.
- **Forgetting to reschedule.** The override is read at planning time, so run Drive Schedule for it to take effect on the plan.

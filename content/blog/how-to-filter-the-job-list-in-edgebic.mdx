**To filter the job list in EDGEBIC, use the search box and Group view toggle on the Drive Schedule grid, and the per-job checkboxes and Search jobs box in the Job View left panel.** Filtering is how you find the work you care about in a grid of hundreds of orders. It is not the same as selecting: EDGEBIC by User Solutions plans the rows you tick, not the rows a filter happens to show, and keeping those two ideas separate is what stops accidental plant-wide runs.

This is a task post. For the run that acts on your selection, read [how to run the scheduler in EDGEBIC](/blog/how-to-run-the-scheduler-in-edgebic), and browse the full library at the [EDGEBIC how-to hub](/blog/edgebic-how-to-hub).

## Before You Start

- You have orders on the **Drive Schedule** grid, or scheduled jobs to view on the **Job View** tab.
- You understand the split between filtering (what you see) and selecting (what a run touches).

## Filter the Orders Grid

1. **Open the Schedule area** and select the **Drive Schedule** tab.
2. **Type into the search box** above the grid. It matches across all columns at once, so a fragment of a job number, a product name or a customer id narrows the grid in one step.
3. **Toggle Group view mode** on the toolbar to nest each job under its parent sales order. The per-job **Status** column then shows the words **Scheduled**, **InProgress**, **Delayed** or **Completed**. Group view is session-only: a restart returns you to the flat grid.

## Filter the Job View Gantt

1. **Open the Job View tab.** The left panel lists jobs and work centers, each job with a checkbox. On first open every job is checked.
2. **Untick jobs** to remove their bars from the Gantt. Work center rows stay visible even when their bars are hidden.
3. **Type into the Search jobs box** to find a job number quickly.
4. **Click Select All Jobs** to re-check everything at once.
5. **Set the date window** with the **Start** and **End** pickers. The default runs from today minus seven days through today plus three weeks. Clear a picker to fall back to the job's own first or last date on that side.

## What Changes When You Filter

| Action | What it changes | What it does not change |
|---|---|---|
| Grid search box | Which order rows are visible | The plan, the selection, the schedule |
| Group view toggle | The grid's layout, flat versus nested | Anything about the underlying orders |
| Job View checkboxes | Which jobs' bars appear on the Gantt | Whether those jobs are scheduled |
| Job View date pickers | The visible timeline window | The jobs' actual planned dates |

Filtering is a lens. Nothing you do with these controls edits an order or moves a bar. That is exactly why they are safe to reach for constantly.

## How to Check It Worked

On the grid, confirm the row count dropped and the rows on screen match your fragment. On the Job View Gantt, confirm only the ticked jobs draw bars and the timeline spans the window you set. If a job you expected is missing from the Gantt, its checkbox is unticked or it falls outside the date window: search for it, tick it, and widen the horizon if needed.

## Common Mistakes

**Confusing the filter with the selection.** Narrowing the grid does not narrow what a run plans. After filtering, tick the specific rows you want, or use **Select All** against the filtered set, and read the confirm dialog before you commit.

**Expecting Group view to persist.** It is session-only. Set it each session if you prefer the sales-order-nested layout.

**Assuming a hidden job is a deleted job.** Unticking a job on the Job View panel only hides its bars. The job is still scheduled and still holds its capacity. Tick it again to bring it back.

**Looking for a job outside the date window.** The Job View Gantt only draws what falls inside the **Start** and **End** pickers. A job scheduled next month will not appear in a window that ends this week. Widen the horizon or clear a picker.

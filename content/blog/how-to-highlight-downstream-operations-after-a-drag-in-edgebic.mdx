**To highlight downstream operations after a drag in EDGEBIC by User Solutions, open Scheduler Configuration, turn on Use Color for Downstream Ops, and save.** After that, dragging a bar on the Gantt recolors the later steps in the same job to the downstream-changed color, typically yellow, so you can see which operations sit after the one you moved. It is a visual cue that those steps may need attention, not an automatic change to their scheduled times. The steps move only when you reschedule.

This is one configuration task. For the base color scheme it layers on, see [how to change the Gantt color coding](/blog/how-to-change-the-gantt-color-coding-in-edgebic). The full task set is on the [EDGEBIC how-to hub](/blog/edgebic-how-to-hub).

## Before You Start

- You can reach Scheduler Configuration, where the toggle lives.
- A job with several steps, so a drag has downstream operations to color.
- An understanding that the color is a cue and does not move any step on its own.

## Step by Step

1. Open **Scheduler Configuration**.
2. Turn on **Use Color for Downstream Ops**.
3. Optionally turn on **Warn On Refresh Downstream Ops** to also see a count of downstream-changed steps when the Gantt loads.
4. Click **Apply** or **Save**.
5. Go to the Job View Gantt and drag a bar in a multi-step job.
6. Watch the later steps in that job recolor to the downstream-changed color.

## What Changes When You Save

With the setting on, each drag marks the steps in the same job that start after the bar you moved. They take the downstream-changed color so the ripple is visible at a glance. A step that is already in an overridden state from its own drag keeps that color rather than being recolored, so your deliberate moves are not masked. Crucially, the coloring is a cue only: the downstream steps keep their scheduled times until you save the drag and run Re-Schedule, which recomputes the plan and realigns them for real.

If you also enable the refresh warning, loading the Gantt shows a count of downstream-changed steps, which is a quick reminder that a prior drag has ripples still to resolve.

## How to Check It Worked

In a job with several steps, drag an early step to a new time. The later steps in that job should recolor to the downstream-changed color. Steps in other jobs should be unaffected, confirming the cascade is scoped to one job. Save and reschedule, and the colors should resolve as the plan recomputes. If nothing recolors, confirm the setting saved and that the job actually has steps after the one you dragged.

## Common Mistakes

- **Reading the color as a completed move.** The color is a warning. The steps move only when you reschedule.
- **Expecting it across jobs.** The cascade is scoped to the job you dragged within, not the whole Gantt.
- **Forgetting your own overrides keep their color.** A step you already moved yourself will not pick up the downstream color.
- **Not saving the configuration.** The toggle takes effect only after Apply or Save.
